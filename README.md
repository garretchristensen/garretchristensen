### Garret Christensen

Economist working on causal inference, large administrative data, and survey methodology. 
Currently a Senior Research Economist at the FDIC; previously at the U.S. Census Bureau and UC Berkeley 
([BITSS](https://www.bitss.org/), [BIDS](https://bids.berkeley.edu/)).

**What I work on**
- Linking survey and administrative records (IRS, deeds, AHS) to measure long-run effects of homeownership
- Survey design for federal household surveys
- Research transparency and reproducibility
